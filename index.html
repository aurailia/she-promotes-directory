A directory of women in business. Find the healer, coach or helper you’ve been looking for.

If you’re looking for a healer, a coach, an Akashic Records reader, a Human Design guide or someone to build your website, you might find her here. Every woman listed is part of She Promotes™, a Skool community where women in business support each other. Created by Aurailia Arnold.

How to use it

  • Search for what you need, or tap a category below to see who offers it.
  • Use the menu to see every offer, the communities these women run, the ones they recommend, and promo communities where you can share your own business.
  • Tap Details to see more, or tap her name to see her full profile.
  • Found someone you love? Book her, join her community, or follow her on social.
  • New to Skool? It’s a free app where most of these communities live. Tap Join the community, sign up with your email, and you’re in. Some communities are free and some are paid. You’ll see which on each one.
