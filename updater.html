Step 2

Bring in your form submissions

In your Google Form, open the responses spreadsheet, then choose File, Download, Comma Separated Values (.csv). Choose that file here. Export all submissions every time. Duplicates are handled for you.

Step 3

Review and approve

Open her Skool profile to check she’s premium, then set her to Live. Pending and Hidden listings stay out of the Directory.

Step 4

Download and upload

  1. Go to your she-promotes-directory repo on GitHub.
  2. Click Add file, then Upload files, and drop in the new directory.json.
  3. Click Commit changes. The Directory updates in a minute or two.
